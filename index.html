🕉️ Comprehensive Pilgrimage Route

Day-by-Day Yatra Itinerary (7D / 6N)

Designed with gradual altitude acclimatization, ITBP checkpost protocols, and sacred darshan timings.

Day 01 · Spiritual Gateway

Kathgodam to Dharchula via Kainchi Dham

Altitude: 915 m / 3,000 ft
Distance280 km (8-9 hrs drive)
StayDharchula Hotel / Guest House
MealsLunch & Dinner
Vehicle4x4 Hill Cruiser

Begin your sacred pilgrimage early morning from Kathgodam Railway Station. Stop at the world-renowned Kainchi Dham Ashram of Neem Karoli Baba in Bhowali for morning blessings. Proceed through the scenic pine valleys of Almora and Pithoragarh, following the course of the Kali River into the border frontier town of Dharchula. Complete medical fitness review and prepare for Inner Line Permit verification.

Key Highlights:

  • Darshan & meditation at Neem Karoli Baba Ashram (Kainchi Dham).
  • Panoramic views of Kali River marking the Indo-Nepal border.
  • Briefing session by your native Kumaoni expedition leader in Dharchula.
Day 02 · Into Higher Himalayas

Dharchula to Gunji / Nabi Village

Altitude: 3,200 m / 10,500 ft
Distance75 km (5-6 hrs drive)
StayAuthentic Kumaoni Homestay (Gunji / Nabi)
MealsBreakfast, Hot Lunch, Sattvic Dinner
Vehicle4x4 Bolero Mountain Fleet

Drive along the newly built high-altitude border road alongside the foaming Kali River. Pass through historical Himalayan hamlets of Malpa, Najang, and ascend through the breathtaking switchbacks of Chialekh Pass. Experience your first dramatic glimpse of alpine meadows carpeted with Himalayan wildflowers. Check-in at the picturesque traditional village of Gunji or Nabi for acclimatization.

Key Highlights:

  • Crossing ITBP checkpoint at Chialekh Pass with verified Inner Line Permits.
  • Dramatic waterfalls and vertical Himalayan rock canyons.
  • Warm welcome with traditional Kumaoni herbal tea in wooden village homestays.
Day 03 · The Sacred Peak

Gunji to Jolingkong, Parvati Sarovar & Adi Kailash

Altitude: 4,550 m / 14,950 ft
Distance35 km one-way (2-3 hrs drive)
StayGunji / Nabi Homestay
MealsBreakfast, Packed/Hot Lunch, Dinner
HighlightChhota Kailash Darshan

Depart at dawn towards Jolingkong base camp. The breathtaking pyramidal peak of Mount Adi Kailash (5,945m) stands before you in sheer majesty. Walk gently to the sacred turquoise waters of Parvati Sarovar and offer prayers at the ancient Shiva-Parvati stone temple. Yatris who wish may trek to the higher glacier pool of Gauri Kund at the very foot of the peak. Return to Nabi for an evening cultural session.

Key Highlights:

  • Spellbinding reflection of Adi Kailash in the calm waters of Parvati Sarovar.
  • Puja and Abhishek at the sacred Shiva Temple by the lake.
  • Optional pilgrimage hike to holy Gauri Kund (foot of Mount Adi Kailash).
Day 04 · The Miraculous OM

Gunji to Nabhidhang & Om Parvat Darshan

Altitude: 4,250 m / 13,940 ft
Distance22 km one-way to Nabhidhang
StayGunji / Nabi Homestay
MealsBreakfast, Lunch, Dinner
HighlightNatural 'ॐ' Snow Formation

Drive along the old Kailash Mansarovar pilgrimage route through Kalapani. Visit the sacred Kali Mata Temple, the divine origin of River Kali, and behold the curious snake-shaped Sheshnag Parvat. Proceed to Nabhidhang for the awe-inspiring darshan of Mount Om Parvat (6,191m), where pristine white snow naturally forms the sacred Hindu symbol 'ॐ' against black rock faces.

Key Highlights:

  • Witnessing the miraculous, natural snow 'ॐ' on Om Parvat with crystal morning skies.
  • Special puja at Kalapani Kali Temple and view of Sheshnag ridge.
  • Viewpoints looking towards Lipulekh Pass on the Tibet-India border.
Day 05 · The Descent

Nabi / Gunji to Dharchula Basecamp

Altitude: 915 m / 3,000 ft
Distance75 km (5 hrs drive)
StayDharchula Hotel
MealsBreakfast, Lunch, Dinner
ActivityDescent & Local Exploration

After breakfast and blessings from the local host family, begin the descent back towards Dharchula. Enjoy the changing Himalayan ecology from alpine meadows to lush sub-tropical forests. Arrive in Dharchula by late afternoon. Stroll across the suspension bridge connecting India and Nepal, and explore local Kumaoni wool markets.

Key Highlights:

  • Farewell felicitation from local village council in Nabi.
  • Visit the Indo-Nepal suspension bridge across the Kali River in Dharchula.
  • Rest and recovery at lower altitude.
Day 06 · Ancient Jyotirlinga

Dharchula to Jageshwar Dham via Pithoragarh

Altitude: 1,870 m / 6,135 ft
Distance190 km (6-7 hrs drive)
StayJageshwar Resort / Guest House
MealsBreakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Highlight124 Ancient Shiva Temples

Travel via the historic valley of Pithoragarh towards Jageshwar Dham, a cluster of over 124 ancient 7th-to-12th century stone temples nestled inside towering deodar cedar forests. Attend the serene evening Ganga Aarti and Rudrabhishek at the sanctum of the Maha Mrityunjaya and Jyotirlinga shrines.

Key Highlights:

  • Visit Jageshwar Jyotirlinga complex in the tranquil cedar groves.
  • Evening Aarti in the presence of ancient stone temple spires.
  • Sacred silence and spiritual rejuvenation.
Day 07 · Completion of Circuit

Jageshwar to Kathgodam via Chitai Golu Devta

Drop-off: Kathgodam Railway Station
Distance125 km (4 hrs drive)
DropKathgodam Station by 4:00 PM
MealsBreakfast & Lunch
HighlightTemple of 10,000 Bells

Conclude your sacred pilgrimage with a visit to the famous Chitai Golu Devta Temple near Almora — known as the God of Justice, where thousands of brass bells hang as testimonies of granted prayers. Drive down to Kathgodam Railway Station in time for evening departures to Delhi (Shatabdi / Ranikhet Express).

Key Highlights:

  • Tie a sacred bell and seek blessings at Chitai Golu Devta Temple.
  • Return with holy Parvati Sarovar Jal and divine memories.
  • Timely drop-off at Kathgodam Railway Station.
ॐ Divine Manifestations

The Sacred Wonders of Adi Kailash & Om Parvat

According to the Skanda Purana and Mahabharata, Lord Shiva meditated here in Vyas Valley before his divine marriage with Goddess Parvati.

Elevation: 5,945 m / 19,505 ft

Mount Adi Kailash (Chhota Kailash)

Known as the primeval abode of Mahadeva, Adi Kailash is one of the five sacred Kailash peaks (Panch Kailash). Nestled in the tranquil Vyas Valley of Uttarakhand, it is revered as the oldest seat of Lord Shiva's eternal meditation.

"He who takes darshan of Adi Kailash attains freedom from the cycles of birth and reaps the fruit of Kailash Mansarovar." — Manaskhand, Skanda Purana
Explore Full Adi Kailash Guide →
Elevation: 6,191 m / 20,311 ft

Mount Om Parvat (The Divine 'ॐ')

A cosmic geological miracle. The black rock fissures and snow deposits naturally etch the exact shape of the sacred Vedic syllable 'ॐ' (AUM) onto the mountain face. Among the eight revered Om peaks in the Himalayas, this is the only one discovered and visible to mortals.

"The primordial sound that resonated at the creation of the universe etched forever in eternal Himalayan snow."
Explore Full Om Parvat Guide →
Elevation: 4,500 m / 14,760 ft

Parvati Sarovar & Shiva Temple

A crystal-clear, high-altitude glacial lake situated at the very base of Mount Adi Kailash in Jolingkong. On its tranquil shores stands an ancient stone shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati, surrounded by fluttering red trishuls and sacred prayer flags.

"Pilgrims perform parikrama and carry the sacred holy water (Sarovar Jal) believed to purify generations of sins."

Inner Line Permit (ILP) & Fitness Guidelines

Because Vyas Valley borders Tibet and Nepal, government regulations require Inner Line Permits (ILP) and ITBP checkpoint clearance. Our team manages all red-tape seamlessly.

1

Online Document Submission

Send soft copies of your Aadhaar Card, passport-size photograph, and medical fitness form to our Pithoragarh team 15 days prior to travel.

2

SDM & Police Verification

Our native team processes your documents directly through the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) office and local police authorities in Dharchula.

3

Original Permit Issuance

Your verified Inner Line Permit is handed over to you in person upon arrival at our Dharchula basecamp before proceeding upstream.

4

ITBP Checkpost Endorsements

Our expedition leader handles all checkpoint stamping with the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) at Chialekh, Gunji, and Jolingkong.

Required Documents Checklist (Indian Citizens)

Foreign nationals and NRIs require special Ministry of External Affairs clearance.

❓ Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, authentic answers to assist your pilgrimage preparation.

The Adi Kailash pilgrimage operates in two annual windows: early summer (May–June) and autumn (September–October). The Autumn 2026 season is currently active and operating through late October, offering crystal-clear blue skies, high visibility, mirror reflections in Parvati Sarovar, and crisp darshan of Om Parvat. Advance pre-registrations are also open for the May–June 2027 season.
We handle the complete permit procedure on your behalf. You simply provide soft copies of your Aadhaar card, doctor fitness certificate, and photos. Our ground team in Dharchula submits the files to the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) office, secures the police clearance, and arranges your original permit prior to your arrival. Read the complete ILP & Permit Guide →
Thanks to the newly completed border roads constructed by the Border Roads Organisation (BRO), 4x4 vehicles can now reach directly up to Jolingkong (Adi Kailash base) and Nabhidhang (Om Parvat viewpoint). From Jolingkong, it is an easy 1.5 km gentle walk to Parvati Sarovar. Horses and mules are also readily available for senior pilgrims. Explore the full Adi Kailash route guide →
Pilgrims between the ages of 9 and 70 years with standard medical fitness are officially permitted (yatris aged 70–75 years require special medical clearance, normal ECG, and physician endorsement). You will need an MBBS Doctor Fitness Certificate certifying you are fit for high altitude travel above 10,000 ft. We provide continuous acclimatization halts and carry medical oxygen onboard all expedition vehicles. View MBBS Medical Fitness Guidelines →
In Gunji and Nabi, pilgrims stay in clean, traditional wooden homestays operated by native Kumaoni families or KMVN tourist rest houses. Rooms feature warm heavy blankets and clean beds. All meals served are 100% pure vegetarian, fresh, hygienic, and cooked sattvic style (dal, rice, rotis, sabzi, soup, and hot herbal ginger tea).
BSNL mobile network works intermittently up to Gunji and Nabi village. Private networks (Jio / Airtel) generally lose connectivity past Dharchula. Our expedition team maintains satellite communication linkages and coordinates with ITBP border checkposts for emergency notifications.
