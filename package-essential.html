Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Essential
5 Days / 4 Nights · Dharchula to Dharchula

The most budget-efficient and flexible yatra package. Designed for devotees and road-trippers arranging their own transport to Dharchula. Full comprehensive darshan of both Mount Adi Kailash and Om Parvat with 4x4 fleet, homestays, and all permits.

⏱️ 5 Days / 4 Nights 📍 Start & End: Dharchula Basecamp 🏔️ Max Alt: 14,950 ft 💰 Direct Price: ₹28,500 / Person 🚙 4x4 Mountain Cruiser Included

Package Overview & Who It Is For

The 5 Days / 4 Nights Essential Package starts and concludes directly at our central headquarters in Dharchula, the border town gateway to the Vyas Valley.

This package is the number one choice for:

  • Pilgrims driving their own cars or motorcycles to Dharchula (secure parking provided).
  • Devotees already exploring Kumaon (Nainital, Almora, Munsiyari, or Pithoragarh) who want to add the sacred darshan.
  • Yatris seeking high spiritual value without paying for long intercity transit drives.

100% Darshan Coverage Guaranteed:

Even though it is 5 days, you do not miss a single sacred site! Both Mount Adi Kailash (Jolingkong & Parvati Sarovar) and Mount Om Parvat (Nabhidhang & Kalapani) are fully explored with leisurely morning darshans.

5-Day Detailed Itinerary

01

Arrival in Dharchula & Document Clearance

Altitude: 915 m Reporting Day
Stay: Hotel / Guest House in Dharchula
Meals: Dinner
Check-in: 12:00 PM onwards

Report to our basecamp office in Dharchula by 2:00 PM. Our coordinator will verify your original Aadhaar card, conduct the mandatory physical fitness check at the local hospital, and process your Inner Line Permit (ILP) with the SDM administration. Evening yatra orientation briefing and dinner.

02

Dharchula to Gunji / Nabi via Chialekh Pass

Altitude: 3,200 m Drive: 75 km (5-6 hrs)
Stay: Traditional Homestay in Gunji / Nabi
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Vehicle: 4x4 Mountain Cruiser

Early morning departure by 4x4 fleet. Follow the roaring Kali River gorge, pass through the historical hamlets of Malpa and Najang, and climb through the hairpins of Chialekh. Present permits at the ITBP checkpost and enter the sacred Vyas Valley. Arrive at Gunji/Nabi village for a warm traditional Kumaoni welcome and acclimatization rest.

03

Gunji to Mount Adi Kailash & Parvati Sarovar

Altitude: 4,550 m Drive: 35 km each way
Stay: Homestay in Gunji / Nabi
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Vehicle: 4x4 Cruiser to Jolingkong

Set out before sunrise towards Jolingkong. Marvel at the dramatic, snow-draped pyramid of Mount Adi Kailash. Perform holy puja and parikrama at the turquoise waters of Parvati Sarovar. Visit the sacred Shiva-Parvati Temple on its bank. Yatris feeling energetic can ascend further towards Gauri Kund before driving back to Nabi for hot dinner.

04

Gunji to Om Parvat (Nabhidhang) & Kalapani Temple

Altitude: 4,250 m Drive: 22 km each way
Stay: Homestay in Gunji / Nabi
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Vehicle: 4x4 Cruiser

Drive along the Kali River towards Nabhidhang viewpoint. Stand in awe as the morning light reveals the miraculous snow-engraved Vedic 'ॐ' on the black rockface of Mount Om Parvat. On return, visit the ancient Kali Mata Mandir at Kalapani, offer prayers at the sacred Vyas Gufa, and view Sheshnag Parvat. Overnight at village homestay.

05

Nabi / Gunji to Dharchula & Departure

Altitude: 915 m Drive: 75 km (5 hrs)
Stay: Yatra Concludes
Meals: Breakfast & Farewell Tea
Drop-off: Dharchula (approx. 01:30 PM)

Begin descent back through Chialekh Pass. Arrive in Dharchula by 1:30 PM. Collect personal luggage/vehicles from our parking facility. You are free to begin your onward journey towards Kathgodam, Pithoragarh, or Munsiyari with memories of a lifetime.

Inclusions & Exclusions

What Is Included

  • Dharchula to Dharchula round-trip in 4x4 mountain cruisers.

  • 100% Inner Line Permit processing and SDM administrative fees.
  • Experienced local driver & mountain guide.

What Is Excluded

  • Transportation from your home city/Kathgodam to Dharchula and return.
  • Pony/mule charges at Parvati Sarovar if utilized.
  • Personal snacks, laundry, or phone calls.
  • Any expenses due to unforeseen road delays or mountain weather.
