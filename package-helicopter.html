VIP Helicopter Adi Kailash & Om Parvat
3 Days / 2 Nights · Pithoragarh Airport Base

Experience divine darshan with zero physical strain. Soar over the majestic snow summits of Panchachuli and land directly in the high-altitude sanctum of Jolingkong and Gunji. Includes priority VIP clearances, on-board medical assistance, and heated alpine camps.

⏱️ 3 Days / 2 Nights 🚁 Charter Helicopter Transfer 👴 Senior Citizen & Family Friendly 💰 VIP Price: ₹72,000 / Person 🩺 Doctor & High-Flow O2 Support

Luxury Aerial Pilgrimage Overview

The VIP Helicopter 3 Days / 2 Nights Yatra is tailored for pilgrims who prioritize maximum comfort, have strict work schedules, or wish to bring elderly parents and senior citizens who cannot endure 200+ kilometers of winding mountain roads.

Operating out of Naini Saini Airport in Pithoragarh, your helicopter flight climbs above the dense pine ridges of Kumaon, providing breathtaking aerial vistas of the five sacred Panchachuli peaks before landing directly at the Jolingkong / Gunji Heli Base.

Why Choose the Helicopter Package:

• Save 4 Days of Mountain Travel: Replaces 16+ hours of rugged road travel with a 25-minute scenic Himalayan flight.
• Dedicated Medical Team: An MBBS physician and oxygen concentrators accompany every group.
• VIP Priority Pass: Express ITBP and SDM permit clearing without standing in queues.
• Premium Heated Alpine Camps: Insulated Swiss tents with electric blankets and customized sattvic meals.

3-Day Express VIP Heli Itinerary

01

Arrival in Pithoragarh & VIP Medical Clearance

Altitude: 1,627 m Briefing Day
Stay: Premium Hotel in Pithoragarh
Meals: Dinner
Base: Naini Saini Airport Area

Arrive in Pithoragarh by road or scheduled flight. Our VIP liaison officer welcomes you at the hotel. Complete the expedited medical fitness check-up, oxygen saturation profiling, and biometric Inner Line Permit clearances. Detailed briefing on high-altitude helicopter protocols followed by a gourmet sattvic dinner.

02

Helicopter Sortie to Jolingkong (Adi Kailash) & Om Parvat

Altitude: 4,550 m Helicopter Sortie
Stay: Premium Alpine Camp Gunji
Meals: Breakfast, Hot Lunch & Dinner
Transport: Charter Chopper + 4x4 Support

Early morning transfer to Naini Saini Airport. Take off in your dedicated helicopter, soaring over snow-capped valleys with spectacular aerial views of Mount Adi Kailash. Land at Jolingkong Heli Base. VIP golf cart / assisted walking transfer to Parvati Sarovar for puja and holy darshan. After darshan, fly to Gunji Heli Base. Board our luxury 4x4 to Nabhidhang to witness the miraculous snow 'ॐ' on Om Parvat. Evening stay at premium alpine camp with heated bedding and continuous oxygen support.

03

Aerial Himalayan Flyback to Pithoragarh & Departure

Altitude: 1,627 m Return Flight
Stay: Tour Concludes
Meals: Breakfast
Arrival: Pithoragarh (approx. 10:30 AM)

Enjoy sunrise over the Vyas Valley with hot herbal tea. Board your return helicopter flight, enjoying close aerial passes of the five Panchachuli peaks. Land at Pithoragarh Airport by 10:30 AM. Receive sacred Prasad and certified Yatra completion certificates before departing for your onward journey.

What Is Excluded

  • Personal gratuities, temple offerings, and optional pujas.

Helicopter Yatra FAQs
