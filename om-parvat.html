Mount Om Parvat
(The Miraculous ॐ · 6,191m)

A cosmic geological wonder of the Himalayas where pristine white snow naturally forms the sacred Vedic syllable 'ॐ' (AUM) across a towering black granite rockface at Nabhidhang.

🏔️ Elevation: 6,191 m (20,311 ft) 📍 Nabhidhang, Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand 🕉️ Natural Snow 'ॐ' Formation 🛡️ ITBP & ILP Clearance Handled

The Cosmic Geological Miracle of the Natural ॐ

Rising majestically to an altitude of 6,191 meters (20,311 ft) near the tri-junction of India, Nepal, and Tibet, Mount Om Parvat is one of the most astonishing spiritual wonders on Earth. When gaze upon the mountain from the viewpoint at Nabhidhang, the permanent dark rock ridges and natural snow crevasses depict the sacred Sanskrit character 'ॐ' (AUM) with astonishing precision.

In Vedic metaphysics, 'Om' is the primordial sound of creation — the cosmic vibration from which the universe emerged, through which it is sustained, and into which it dissolves. To see this sacred sound physically inscribed in towering mountain snow is considered an experience that evokes tears of reverence from pilgrims.

The Mystery of Ashta Kailash (The 8 Om Mountains):

According to ancient Hindu texts, there are eight mountains across the Himalayas that bear the divine pattern of Om (known collectively as Ashta Om Parvat). However, among all eight celestial peaks, this mountain in Pithoragarh is the ONLY one that has been discovered, mapped, and made accessible to human darshan.

Unlike typical trekking peaks, Mount Om Parvat has never been scaled to its summit. Out of profound religious reverence for its sacred manifestation, both the Government of India and the local Kumaoni communities hold the mountain as inviolable, ensuring that human footprints never tread upon the holy symbol of Mahadev.

Sacred Spots Around Mount Om Parvat

The pilgrimage trail leading up to Om Parvat passes through some of the most spiritually charged border frontiers of Uttarakhand:

1. Nabhidhang Viewpoint

Altitude: 4,250 m / 13,940 ft

The primary observation plateau situated right opposite Mount Om Parvat. From here, yatris sit in silent dhyana (meditation) and witness the rising sun illuminating the white snow-covered ॐ against the dark cliff.

2. Kalapani Kali Mandir

Altitude: 3,600 m / 11,810 ft

The divine birthplace and origin of the sacred Kali River (which flows downstream forming the international boundary with Nepal). An ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Kali stands here where pilgrims offer prayers and receive blessings from ITBP jawans.

3. Sheshnag Parvat

Overlooking Kalapani

Rising directly behind the Kalapani river springs is an extraordinary mountain ridge that resembles the coiled, multi-hooded divine serpent Sheshnag, who shelters Lord Vishnu in the cosmic ocean.

4. Lipulekh Pass

Elevation: 5,200 m / 17,060 ft

The historic mountain pass connecting India with Tibet, traditionally used for centuries by the official Kailash Mansarovar Yatra. Yatris can view the ancient trade paths winding into the high Tibetan plateau.

How to Reach & Best Time for OM Darshan

The newly completed border highway by the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) allows 4x4 vehicles to drive directly up to Nabhidhang from Dharchula and Gunji:

Om Parvat Route:

Dharchula → Chialekh Pass → Gunji / Nabi Village (Acclimatization Base) → Kalapani (Kali Temple & Sheshnag View) → Nabhidhang (Om Parvat Viewpoint). Total distance from Gunji is just 22 km.

The Golden Hour Darshan:

The most breathtaking darshan of Om Parvat happens between 6:30 AM and 9:30 AM. As the morning sun rises over the eastern ridgelines of Nepal, the sun rays hit the southern snow face of Om Parvat at an angle, making the pure white 'ॐ' blaze like polished silver against the dark mountain stone.

Best Season to Visit Om Parvat (2026 Season)

  • May to June: Excellent clarity with crisp morning skies and full snow coverage defining the bold lines of the 'ॐ' symbol.
  • September to October: Deep azure Himalayan skies with near-zero cloud cover. The contrast between dark rock and the snow-filled fissures is at its sharpest. Temperatures range from 4°C to 12°C in daytime and drop below freezing at night.
