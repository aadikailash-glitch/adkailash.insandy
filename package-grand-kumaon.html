Grand Kumaon & Panchachuli Expedition
8 Days / 7 Nights · Adi Kailash, Om Parvat & Darma Valley

The ultimate Himalayan odyssey. Witness the sacred abodes of Shiva in Vyas Valley, then cross into the untouched paradise of Darma Valley to stand face-to-face with the five majestic snow towers of Panchachuli Peaks from Dugtu and Dantu alpine meadows.

⏱️ 8 Days / 7 Nights 🏔️ Covers Vyas Valley & Darma Valley ⛰️ Front-row View of 5 Panchachuli Peaks 💰 Grand Price: ₹46,000 / Person 🚙 Dedicated 4x4 Mountain Expedition Fleet

Expedition Overview: The Dual Valley Wonder

The Grand Kumaon & Panchachuli Expedition (8 Days / 7 Nights) is crafted for passionate explorers, nature photographers, and dedicated devotees who want to experience the complete grandeur of the Eastern Kumaon Himalayas in a single, seamless expedition.

While standard yatras only visit the Vyas Valley, this expedition connects two of Uttarakhand's most pristine border valleys:

Two Legendary Himalayan Valleys in One Trip:

1. Vyas Valley: The sacred realm of Lord Shiva — Mount Adi Kailash (5,945m), Parvati Sarovar, Gauri Kund, and the miraculous snow-inscribed Om Parvat (6,191m).
2. Darma Valley: The untouched wilderness where the Pandavas cooked their final earthly meal (Panch Chuli) on their journey to heaven. Home to high-altitude Rung settlements of Dugtu and Dantu directly facing the mammoth Panchachuli glacier.

8-Day Expedition Itinerary

01

Kathgodam to Dharchula via Kainchi Dham

Altitude: 915 m Drive: 280 km (8-9 hrs)
Stay: Hotel in Dharchula
Meals: Lunch & Dinner
Transport: Hill Cruiser

Pickup at Kathgodam Railway Station. Visit the sacred Kainchi Dham of Neem Karoli Baba. Travel through Almora and Pithoragarh along the winding banks of the Kali River into the border frontier of Dharchula. Document verification for both Vyas & Darma Valley permits.

02

Dharchula to Gunji / Nabi (Vyas Valley Gateway)

Altitude: 3,200 m Drive: 75 km (5-6 hrs)
Stay: Village Homestay in Gunji/Nabi
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Transport: 4x4 Mountain Cruiser

Ascend through the dramatic switchbacks of Chialekh Pass. Pass through ITBP security verification and enter the picturesque plateau of Vyas Valley. Check-in to traditional wooden homestay in Gunji or Nabi for acclimatization.

03

Adi Kailash & Parvati Sarovar Sacred Darshan

Altitude: 4,550 m Drive: 35 km each way
Stay: Homestay in Gunji/Nabi
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Transport: 4x4 Cruiser to Jolingkong

Drive to Jolingkong Basecamp for morning darshan of the grand pyramid of Mount Adi Kailash (5,945 m). Parikrama and holy Jal collection at Parvati Sarovar; optional hike to Gauri Kund. Return to Nabi village for evening celebration and hot sattvic dinner.

04

Om Parvat (Nabhidhang) & Kalapani Temple

Altitude: 4,250 m Drive: 22 km each way
Stay: Homestay in Gunji/Nabi
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Transport: 4x4 Cruiser

Witness the cosmic snow miracle of Mount Om Parvat at sunrise from Nabhidhang. Visit the holy Kali Mata Mandir at Kalapani and see the underground spring headwaters of the Kali River. Overnight at Gunji.

05

Vyas Valley to Darma Valley (Dugtu / Dantu Village)

Altitude: 3,350 m Cross-Valley Drive
Stay: Traditional Homestay in Dugtu / Dantu
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Transport: 4x4 Mountain Cruiser

Descend to Tawaaghat and branch off into the untamed Dhauliganga river gorge entering the secluded Darma Valley. Drive past Sela, Baaling, and arrive at the high alpine hamlets of Dugtu and Dantu. The mammoth 5 peaks of Panchachuli rise dramatically right across the meadow! Check-in to authentic local homestay.

06

Panchachuli Glacier Base Exploration & Darma Trails

Altitude: 3,800 m Alpine Walk / 4x4
Stay: Traditional Homestay in Dugtu / Dantu
Meals: Breakfast, Hot Lunch & Dinner
Activity: Glacier Base Trail & Photography

Wake up to the golden alpenglow illuminating all 5 peaks of Panchachuli (highest 6,904 m). Enjoy an invigorating walk across the alpine meadows towards the snout of the Panchachuli glacier. Explore traditional carved wooden doors and stone architecture of Dantu village and experience local Rung cultural hospitality.

07

Darma Valley to Munsiyari / Birthi Falls

Altitude: 2,200 m Drive: 140 km (6 hrs)
Stay: Resort / Hotel in Munsiyari
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Transport: 4x4 Mountain Cruiser

Bid farewell to Darma Valley. Drive down via Dharchula and ascend towards the picturesque hill station of Munsiyari. Visit the spectacular 126-meter cascade of Birthi Falls en route. Enjoy sunset views of Panchachuli from a different panoramic angle.

08

Munsiyari to Kathgodam Drop-off

Altitude: 550 m Drive: 275 km (8 hrs)
Stay: Journey Concludes
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Arrival: Kathgodam (approx. 05:00 PM)

Drive back through Thal, Berinag, and Almora. Drop-off at Kathgodam Railway Station by 5:00 PM in time for your evening trains back to Delhi.
